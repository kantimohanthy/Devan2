RFC 1035 Iterative DNS Referral Analysis DERIVED STATE: TESTED
Source Anchor RFC 1035 — Domain Names: Implementation and Specification
Hypothesis & Question How many referral hops does dig +trace execute from root to authoritative server, and is RD=0 sent on iterative queries?
Method dig +trace example.com captured simultaneously with tshark -i eth0 -f "port 53" -w dns-trace.pcap
Observed Result Confirmed hypothesis. Verified 4 distinct iterative referral hops (Root -> TLD -> Authoritative -> Final IN A record 93.184.215.14) with RD=0 header flag.
Diagnosis & What Surprised Initial capture missed Hop 1 due to systemd-resolved loopback cache on 127.0.0.53:53; resolved via -i any interface flag.
